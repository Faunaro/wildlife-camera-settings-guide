# Faunaro — Wildlife Photography Camera Field Guide & Optical Simulator

> Faunaro is a 100% offline wildlife photography field guide and optical simulator app for iOS, Android, and Web. It translates 14 real-world wildlife photography scenarios (such as Birds in Flight, Dawn/Dusk Hides, Fast Mammals, Macro/Amphibians) into exact, firmware-verified camera menu paths, autofocus tracking parameters, exposure settings, and custom button assignments for 81 mirrorless & DSLR cameras across Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, OM System (Olympus), and Leica, paired with 178 telephoto lenses.

## Key Features for Wildlife Photographers
1. **730 Firmware-Verified Original Menu Paths:** Exact menu locations in both German and English firmware (e.g., Sony `AF/MF › AF-Nachführ-Empfindlichkeit / AF Subj. Shift Sens.`, Canon `AF (Magenta) › Tab 2 › Servo AF Case 1–4 / Auto`, Nikon `Individualfunktionen (Stift) › a3 Schärfenachf. mit Lock-On`, OM System `Pro Capture Low/High`, Fujifilm `Pre-Shot ES`).
2. **Optical Depth-of-Field (DoF) & Close-Focus Calculator:** Calculates exact hyperfocal distance, near/far focus limits, and depth of field in millimeters/centimeters for 81 sensors (Full Frame, APS-C, Micro Four Thirds) and 178 telephoto/macro lenses including 1.4x and 2.0x teleconverters.
3. **Interactive 3:2 Telephoto Viewfinder Simulator:** Simulates real framing and subject fill % for 11 wildlife species (e.g., Kingfisher, Red Fox, White-tailed Eagle, Red Deer) from 100mm to 1200mm across sensor crop modes.
4. **My Custom Keys (Button Mapping Guide):** Tailored physical button assignments (`AF-ON`, `AEL`, `C1–C4`, `Fn`, Lens Function Ring) for instant Dual Back-Button AF switching in the field.
5. **True 620nm Red-Light Night Mode:** Protects human rhodopsin (night adaptation) in dark wildlife hides, dawn blinds, and nocturnal owl/bat photography without spooking shy wildlife.
6. **100% Offline & Fair Pricing:** Works completely without cellular reception in remote forests, mountains, and national parks. Core guide is free; Faunaro Pro is a $1.99 / 1,99 € one-time purchase (no subscription, zero tracking).
