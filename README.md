Faunaro — Wildlife Photography Camera Field Guide & Optical Simulator

Faunaro is a 100% offline wildlife photography field guide and optical simulator app for iOS, Android, and Web. It translates 14 real-world wildlife photography scenarios (such as Birds in Flight, Dawn/Dusk Hides, Fast Mammals, Macro/Amphibians) into exact, firmware-verified camera menu paths, autofocus tracking parameters, exposure settings, and custom button assignments for 108 mirrorless & DSLR cameras across Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, OM System (Olympus), Panasonic Lumix, and Leica, paired with 226 telephoto lenses.
